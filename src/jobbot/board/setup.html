This job board isn't set up yet

Once it is, this page shows the jobs that match you, refreshed every few hours and encrypted with a passphrase only you know. It's free: everything runs on GitHub Actions and GitHub Pages.

  1. Write your config

    Either run jobbot on your computer, fill in your profile and sources, and copy the text from Settings → Hosted job board, or copy the example config and fill in your details: target titles, skills, years of experience, the companies you'd like to work for and a few job searches.

  2. Add two repository secrets

    In Settings → Secrets and variables → Actions, add:

    • JOBBOT_CONFIG: your config from step 1.
    • JOBBOT_PAGE_PASSWORD: a long passphrase (at least 12 characters; four or five random words work well). GitHub Pages sites are public, so your board is encrypted with it.

    Optional: ADZUNA_APP_ID and ADZUNA_APP_KEY (free) or JSEARCH_API_KEY (free tier) for more job sources, and ANTHROPIC_API_KEY (paid) for Claude's read on your interview chances. Without it, jobs get the built-in estimated score.

  3. Run the workflow

    Open Actions → Job board and click Run workflow. When it finishes, reload this page and unlock it with your passphrase. After that it updates on its own every 6 hours.
